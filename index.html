⚙️ How the connection works

Designed for multiple independent WhatsApp sessions.
01 · CREATE

Orihime creates an isolated WAHA session for one linked WhatsApp account.

02 · PAIR

Enter the generated code in WhatsApp → Linked devices → Link a device → Link with phone number.

03 · FOLLOW

The backend automatically follows the configured Orihime channel and syncs media/captions.

04 · RECOVER

The watchdog restarts stopped/failed sessions without calling logout/delete during normal recovery.
